Winter evenings

Yarning at The Vine

Veterans' stories by the fire. On winter evenings the open fire at The Vine is lit, the room fills up, and veterans of the British Armed Forces tell stories from their lives, followed by questions from the room.

By the fire

Two or three veterans take turns by the fire, each telling a yarn of about twenty minutes. It might be about a first posting, a hard winter, an old friendship, or the day things went wrong and what came after.

Good company

It isn't a lecture, a parade or a politics lesson. It's good stories told well by people who were there. There's an interval for the bar and kitchen, then questions from the room.

Where it started

The pilot Veterans' Story Hour was held here at The Vine on Monday 14 September 2026. Yarning brings the same spirit to winter evenings in the pub.
