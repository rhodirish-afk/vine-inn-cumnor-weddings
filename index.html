A timeless setting for unforgettable celebrations

Nestled in the heart of picturesque Cumnor, The Vine Inn is a charming, historic village pub – the perfect backdrop for intimate weddings and private events.

Just 10 minutes from Oxford and about 50 minutes by train from London. Traditional character throughout – exposed stone walls, dark beams, an oak bar and an open fire – plus a large, beautifully kept beer garden.

  • ✓ Exclusive use of the whole pub & garden
  • ✓ Two fully serviced bars & late licence available
  • ✓ Seated up to 40 · Buffets & BBQs up to 200
