A timeless setting for unforgettable celebrations

Nestled in the heart of picturesque Cumnor, The Vine Inn is a charming historic traditional village pub – the perfect backdrop for intimate weddings and private events.

Just 10 minutes from Oxford and ~50 minutes by train from London. Traditional character with open stone walls, dark beams, oak bar, open fire, and a large beautifully maintained beer garden.
